## About DreamLab
Mercury DreamLab 是一个围绕 Iconic Mercury 建立的社区驱动内容俱乐部，鼓励深度、大胆的修改——实验性内容、全面转换、游戏、演示，以及超越常规想象的想法。

DreamLab 是 Mercury Engine 的项目倡议，并非官方 IX‑Ray 社区。它非商业、由社区驱动，致力于以合法、尊重、非商业的方式把 Iconic Mercury 推向更远处。
> Mercury DreamLab is a community‑driven content club built around Iconic Mercury. It encourages deep, ambitious modifications—experiments, total conversions, games, demos, and ideas that go beyond conventional limits.
>
> DreamLab is a Mercury Engine initiative, not an official IX‑Ray community. It is non‑commercial, community‑driven, and focused on pushing Iconic Mercury further—legally, respectfully, and without commercial exploitation.

## Open Access | 开放访问
DreamLab 面向所有拥有 Iconic Mercury 访问权限的人开放。这些工具以及 DreamLab 公开提供的内容均平等、免费、无限制提供，不需要购买、捐赠、订阅或任何物质贡献。
> DreamLab is open to everyone who has access to Iconic Mercury. These tools and DreamLab’s openly provided content are available equally, for free, and without limits. No purchase, donation, subscription, or material contribution is required.

## Membership | 成员资格
DreamLab 正式成员由创始人亲自选择。申请者必须证明自己有能力将想法发展为扎实的项目，并帮助维持 DreamLab 向其他用户公开提供的内容质量。加入不需要任何物质贡献——只需要信任、值得支持的认真想法，以及对以下规则的完全遵守。
> Formal DreamLab membership is personally selected by the founder. To become a member, a user must demonstrate the ability to develop their idea into a solid project and help maintain the quality of content that DreamLab provides openly to other users. Joining requires no material contribution—only trust, a serious idea worth supporting, and full compliance with the rules listed below.

## Rules for Members | 成员规约
### What members must comply with | 成员必须遵守
1. 成员的一切行为与项目分发必须遵守 MFL，该许可证严格禁止任何形式的商业活动。
> All member behavior and project distribution must comply with the MFL, which strictly forbids commercial activity of any kind.

2. 成员不得以任何形式出售 Iconic Mercury 的任何内容。
> Members must not, in any form, sell any content of Iconic Mercury.

3. 成员不得将以任何形式获得的、与 Iconic Mercury 相关的内容用于 MFL 未允许的用途。
> Members must not use content obtained in any form related to Iconic Mercury outside what the MFL permits.

4. 对成员的自愿捐赠必须真正自愿，且不得提供或承诺任何形式的回报。捐赠不能购买成员资格、访问权限、优先级、影响力或专属内容。
> Voluntary donations to members must be truly voluntary and must not provide or promise any kind of return. Donations cannot buy membership, access, priority, influence, or exclusive content.

### What members may do | 成员允许进行
1. 根据个人选择接受自愿捐赠，无论来自社区还是创始人，但必须遵守“无回报”原则。
> Accept voluntary donations at their personal discretion, whether from the community or from the founders, provided the no‑return rule is respected.

2. 每月，DreamLab 会向评价最积极的项目提供由创始人提供的官方捐赠。
> Every month, DreamLab offers official donations from the founders to the most positively rated projects.

3. 在 DreamLab 之外再分发或分享自己的项目，但必须遵守 MFL，明确署名 Iconic Mercury，并附上所有必需的许可证声明。
> Redistribute or share their projects outside DreamLab under the MFL, with clear credit to Iconic Mercury and all required license notices included.

## Founder Donations & Redistribution | 创始人捐赠与资金再分配
1. 创始人提供的捐赠来源于创始人自有财产，或创始人从社区接受的自愿捐赠。
> Donations made by the founders are sourced from the founders’ own property or from donations accepted by the founders from the community.

2. 创始人承诺：每月从社区接受的自愿捐赠中，至少 25% 将再分配给 DreamLab 成员（如有）。
> The founders promise that at least **25%** of community donations accepted monthly will be redistributed to DreamLab members, if any.

3. 若某月因缺乏适用项目或违反 MFL 而未进行官方捐赠，该 25% 将由创始人保留，并公开记录在 DreamLab 的 MCLF 中。所有 Iconic Mercury 用户与 DreamLab 成员均可审计 MCLF 的收入与支出。
> If no official donations are made in a given month due to a lack of applicable projects or violations of the MFL, that 25% will be retained by the founders and openly recorded in DreamLab’s MCLF. All users of Iconic Mercury and all DreamLab members may audit MCLF’s income and expenses.

4. 对 MCLF 的直接捐赠 100% 保留在 MCLF 之下。创始人负责确保 MCLF 的收入与支出不被用于任何形式的个人用途。
> Direct donations to MCLF are kept 100% under MCLF. The founders are responsible for ensuring that MCLF’s income and expenses are not used for any form of personal use.

5. 每月，MCLF 将捐出其已收到捐赠的至少 10%、至多 20%。
> Every month, MCLF will give away at least **10%** and at most **20%** of the donations it has received.
