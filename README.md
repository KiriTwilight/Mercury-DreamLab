## Definitions
> 中文释义：
> - **Iconic Mercury**：统称 Mercury Engine（ME）、Mercury Universal Editor（MUE）、Mercury Universal Launcher（MUL）及其相关组成部分，包括其源代码、二进制文件、资产与文档等。
> - **MFL**：Mercury Freeport License（水星自由港许可协议）
> - **MCLF**：Mercury Community Love Foundation（水星社区爱心基金）

In this document:
- **Iconic Mercury**: Collectively refers to Mercury Engine (ME), Mercury Universal Editor (MUE), and Mercury Universal Launcher (MUL).
- **MFL**: Mercury Freeport License
- **MCLF**: Mercury Community Love Foundation

## About DreamLab
Mercury DreamLab 是围绕 Iconic Mercury 搭建、由社区自主驱动的内容创作俱乐部。我们鼓励富有深度、大胆突破的改造项目：实验内容、完整模组转换、独立游戏、技术演示，以及一切挣脱常规边界的创意构想。

DreamLab 属于 Mercury Engine 发起项目，**并非 IX‑Ray 官方社区**。本组织为非营利性质、社区自治，目标是在合法、互相尊重、杜绝商业化利用的前提下，拓展 Iconic Mercury 的可能性边界。
> Mercury DreamLab is a community‑driven content club built around Iconic Mercury. It encourages deep, ambitious modifications — experiments, total conversions, games, demos, and ideas that go beyond conventional limits.
>
> DreamLab is a Mercury Engine initiative, **not an official IX‑Ray community**. It is non‑commercial, community‑driven, and focused on pushing Iconic Mercury further — legally, respectfully, and without commercial exploitation.

## Open Access | 开放访问
凡是拥有 Iconic Mercury 使用权限的用户，均可访问 DreamLab。
相关工具以及 DreamLab 对外公开的全部内容，平等、免费、无限制开放。
无需购买、捐赠、订阅，也不强制任何形式的物质贡献。
> DreamLab is open to everyone who has access to Iconic Mercury.
> These tools and DreamLab’s openly provided content are available equally, for free, and without limits.
> No purchase, donation, subscription, or material contribution is required.

## Membership | 成员资格
DreamLab 正式成员由创始人遴选。
申请者需要证明自己有能力将创意落地为稳定、完整的项目，并一同维护 DreamLab 对外公开内容的质量标准。

加入无需物质付出，仅需要彼此信任、一个值得扶持的严肃创作构想，并且完全遵守下文全部规则。
> Formal DreamLab membership is personally selected by the founder.
> To become a member, a user must demonstrate the ability to develop their idea into a solid project and help maintain the quality of content that DreamLab provides openly to other users.
>
> Joining requires no material contribution — only trust, a serious idea worth supporting, and full compliance with the rules listed below.

## Rules for Members | 成员规约
### What members must comply with | 成员必须遵守
1. 成员所有行为与项目分发，必须遵守 MFL；该协议严格禁止一切商业行为。
> All member behavior and project distribution must comply with the **MFL**, which strictly forbids commercial activity of any kind.

2. 成员不得以任何形式售卖 Iconic Mercury 的相关内容。
> Members must not, in any form, sell any content of Iconic Mercury.

3. 成员不得将所有和 Iconic Mercury 相关的内容，用于 MFL 许可范围之外的场景。
> Members must not use content obtained in any form related to Iconic Mercury outside what the MFL permits.

4. 流向成员的自愿捐赠必须完全自愿，不得承诺、提供任何回报。捐赠不能换取成员身份、访问权限、优先权、话语权或者专属内容。
> Voluntary donations to members must be truly voluntary and must not provide or promise any kind of return. Donations cannot buy membership, access, priority, influence, or exclusive content.

### What members may do | 成员允许进行
1. 在遵守“无回报”原则的前提下，成员可自行决定是否接受来自社区或创始人的自愿捐赠。
> Accept voluntary donations at their personal discretion, whether from the community or from the founders, provided the no‑return rule is respected.

2. 每月 DreamLab 会由创始人出资，对口碑优秀的项目发放官方激励捐赠。
> Every month, DreamLab offers official donations from the founders to the most positively rated projects.

3. 成员可以在 DreamLab 以外传播、分发自己的项目，但必须遵循 MFL，标注 Iconic Mercury 归属，附带完整许可声明。
> Redistribute or share their projects outside DreamLab under the MFL, with clear credit to Iconic Mercury and all required license notices included.

## Founder Donations & Redistribution | 创始人捐赠与资金再分配
1. 创始人发放的捐赠资金，来自创始人自有资产，或是创始人接收的社区自愿捐赠。
> Donations made by the founders are sourced from the founders’ own property or from donations accepted by the founders from the community.

2. 创始人承诺：每月收到的社区捐赠总额中，至少 25% 会分配给 DreamLab 成员（符合条件时）。
> The founders promise that at least **25%** of community donations accepted monthly will be redistributed to DreamLab members, if any.

3. 若当月无合格项目，或存在 MFL 违规，该 25% 份额转入 MCLF 记账留存。所有 Iconic Mercury 用户、DreamLab 成员均有权审计 MCLF 的收支明细。
> If no official donations are made in a given month due to a lack of applicable projects or violations of the MFL, that 25% will be retained by the founders and openly recorded in DreamLab’s MCLF. All Iconic Mercury users and all DreamLab members may audit MCLF’s income and expenses.

4. 直接捐赠至 MCLF 的资金全额归入基金账户。创始人负有监督责任，保证 MCLF 资金不会被用于私人用途。
> Direct donations to MCLF are kept 100% under MCLF. The founders are responsible for ensuring that MCLF’s income and expenses are not used for any form of personal use.

5. MCLF 每月将支出当月收到捐赠总额的 10% ~ 20% 用于发放。
> Every month, MCLF will give away at least **10%** and at most **20%** of the donations it has received.
